Gmail MCP Personal

Privacy policy

This connector is operated for its owner's personal use. It accesses only accounts the owner explicitly authorizes.

Data accessed and use

Google sign-in supplies an email address and account identifier to verify the owner's identity. Gmail access supplies the messages, headers, labels, and drafts needed to carry out requests from the connected MCP client. Sending and label changes are available only when enabled by the owner.

Storage and retention

Google credentials, refresh tokens, account identifiers, and connector authorization state are stored encrypted on the owner's server. Mail content is processed in memory for each request and is not saved in the connector's state database. The connector does not log message subjects, bodies, or tokens.

Credentials remain until the owner removes them. Removing an account deletes its stored mailbox authorization; configuration and private backups may retain account identifiers or older credentials until the owner removes those copies. Gmail messages and drafts remain in Google until separately deleted.

Sharing

Requested mail content and tool results are sent to the connected client, such as Claude operated by Anthropic. The client's own policies govern its storage and use of that content. Google processes Gmail and sign-in requests. Network and hosting providers may process traffic and connection metadata while delivering the service.

The connector does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Gmail MCP Personal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and contact

The owner can remove account credentials, revoke connector access, and revoke Google authorization through Google Account connections. Revoking access does not delete prior client conversations or drafts already stored in Gmail.

For access or deletion requests, contact the server operator using the support email displayed on this app's Google authorization screen.
