Gmail MCP Personal

A self-hosted connector that lets its owner use Claude or Codex with their own Gmail accounts.

The connector can search and read messages, create drafts, and, when enabled by its owner, send existing drafts and apply labels. Each request selects an account by its configured alias.

Access requires the owner's Google sign-in and approval. This is a private personal service with no public registration.

Mail content requested through the connector is returned to the connected client. Review the privacy information before connecting an account.
